OUR STORY

About This Awesome Korean BBQ Restaurant

Welcome to MenuSifu! It is a Korean BBQ restaurant located at 349 5th Ave Floor 3, New York, NY 10016. We are committed to bringing authentic Korean BBQ flavors to diners, with a focus on premium, high-quality meats and traditional seasonings that highlight the rich, savory taste of classic Korean cuisine. Our restaurant’s main offerings include marinated galbi (short ribs), juicy bulgogi, flavorful samgyeopsal (pork belly), and a variety of homemade banchan (side dishes) that complement every grill meal perfectly. Come to MenuSifu and enjoy a lively, interactive Korean BBQ experience—gather with friends and family to grill, share, and savor the best of Korean flavors!
